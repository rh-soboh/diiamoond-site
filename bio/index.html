01 — CHAPTER

The beginning

Born in Italy in 2003, Diiamoond grew up surrounded by music in the church.

Rhythm, melody, songwriting and the emotional power of sound were part of his earliest frame of reference. Afrobeats and hip-hop became central influences, and a deep interest in how records are made became a career in production.

02 — CHAPTER

Across borders

His professional journey began in Italy, then travelled to the UK and wider international markets.

Working across different scenes, Diiamoond built a catalogue with artists from different backgrounds. His move to the United Kingdom became a significant chapter, including work on Mimi Webb’s “24/5,” featured on Seven Shades of Heartbreak.

03 — CHAPTER

The collaborators

A growing catalogue connects Diiamoond with artists across pop, hip-hop and global music.

Confirmed collaborations include Mimi Webb, Kap G, K CAMP, GoldLink, Shakka, Tommy Kuti, Yves the Male and Slings, among others. The work moves between sounds and cultures while keeping a strong connection to contemporary African and global music.

04 — CHAPTER

Beyond the record

The production work has also reached film and television.

Diiamoond is credited on “Closure” by Lorne Balfe, placed in Luther: The Fallen Sun (Netflix, 2023). CTRL ROOM extends that collaborative practice into a production-camp tour created to bring producers, songwriters and creatives into the same space.
